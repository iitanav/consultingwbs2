About Us

Who we are

We are trying to foster a collaborative community that empowers NYUAD students to solve business problems, make strategic connections, and launch successful careers in management consulting and beyond.

Welcome to NYUAD The Consulting Club, the premier student-led organization at New York University Abu Dhabi dedicated to developing the next generation of global strategists, innovators, and business leaders.

We bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world business strategy.
